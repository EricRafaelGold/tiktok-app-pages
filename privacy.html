Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

WhitePhoenix ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is a video scheduling and automation platform that allows content creators to connect their TikTok accounts and automatically publish videos on a schedule. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to WhitePhoenix, we receive an OAuth access token issued by TikTok. This token allows us to post content to your TikTok profile on your behalf. We do not receive or store your TikTok username or password. We may also collect basic usage information such as scheduling preferences and posting history within the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to operate the platform — specifically to schedule and publish your videos to TikTok at the times you specify. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than providing the scheduling service you requested.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties, except as required to operate the service (such as communicating with TikTok's official API to publish your content). All data shared with TikTok is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and scheduling data are stored securely. We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access. You can revoke WhitePhoenix's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's account settings.

5. Third-Party Services

WhitePhoenix integrates with TikTok via their official Developer API. By using our platform, you also agree to TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for TikTok's data practices.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. You may also revoke platform access through TikTok's connected apps settings, which will immediately stop all scheduled posting.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
